A bit about me

I started out installing wifi and kept running into the same thing: unused cables piled up on the side of the house like a nest. So I started cleaning them up, using ethernet for a solid wifi connection and putting all the wiring in the crawlspace. When I'm not on a job, you'll find me on a hiking trail, in a sauna or on a soccer field. I take the same care with your home that I'd want for my own: show up on time, do it right, and leave things cleaner than I found them.
